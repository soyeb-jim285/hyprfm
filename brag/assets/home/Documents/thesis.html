On the Optimal Value of gaps_in

A longitudinal study of five versus six pixels

A thesis submitted in partial fulfilment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Procrastination

Department of Dotfiles, University of /home

Abstract

We investigate whether increasing gaps_in from 5 to 6 improves perceived productivity. Over twelve months and 183 reloads we find that productivity is unaffected, regret is substantial, and the window gap was never actually visible. Future work: the same, with blur.

1. Method

Each week the author set gaps_in to a value in {4, 5, 6} and recorded mood on a scale of 1 to 10. Measurements were taken while not writing this thesis.
